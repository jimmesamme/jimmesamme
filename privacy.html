Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy describes how a personal Home Assistant installation accesses and uses Google Nest data through the Google Nest Smart Device Management API.

Information accessed

After Google account authorization, Home Assistant can access only the Nest devices and capabilities selected and permitted during the authorization process. Depending on the devices and permissions, this can include thermostat modes, temperature and humidity readings, camera streams, and camera or doorbell events and related media.

How information is used

The information is used to show device status and to run household automations configured in Home Assistant. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Home Assistant stores its configuration, authorization credentials, and device state on the computer running the Home Assistant installation. Google processes authentication and API requests and delivers Nest events through its cloud services. Google’s handling of information is also subject to Google’s privacy terms. Any optional Home Assistant Cloud, backup, or other third-party service enabled by the household may process information according to that service’s own terms.

Sharing and retention

This personal setup does not intentionally disclose Nest data to other people. Information may be shared with household members who can access Home Assistant and with services explicitly enabled in its configuration. Data is retained in Home Assistant until it is removed through Home Assistant’s history, storage, or backup controls, or the installation is deleted. Google retains information according to its own policies and service settings.

Security and choices

Access can be revoked through the Google account’s third-party connections settings or the Google Nest Partner Connections Manager. Removing the Nest integration from Home Assistant removes its integration configuration, but backups may retain earlier copies.

Contact

This is a personal household project. For privacy questions, contact the household owner through the contact method provided to you directly.
